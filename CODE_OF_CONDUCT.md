# Code of Conduct

## The short version

**Be kind. Assume good faith. Remember that someone asking a basic question is doing the brave thing.**

This is a learning repository. People will arrive confused, and that is the point. Meeting confusion with
condescension is the one thing that will get you removed from this project, no matter how good your code is.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What good behaviour looks like here

- Answering "why is recall@1 so low?" with an explanation, not a link to a paper and a sigh.
- Saying "I think this is wrong, here is a counter-example" instead of "this is wrong".
- Accepting that someone's measurement disagrees with your intuition, and asking for their setup.
- Giving feedback on the work, not the person. Receiving it the same way.
- Crediting ideas you build on, including ideas from issues that did not become PRs.
- Writing in a second language and being met with help, not corrections.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks, public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention of any kind.
- Dismissing or mocking someone for not knowing something.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or deliberately derailing a thread.
- Any other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, code comments —
and when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it by opening an issue marked **"Code of
Conduct"**, or by contacting the maintainer privately through
[GitHub](https://github.com/AlexandreRL). Reports are handled confidentially, and you will get a response.

The maintainer may warn, temporarily block, or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour violates this code.
Enforcement decisions will be explained to the person affected.

If you are reporting in good faith, you will not be retaliated against — including if the person you are
reporting is the maintainer.
